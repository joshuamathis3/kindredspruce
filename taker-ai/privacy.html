01

Overview

Taker AI ("the App") is developed and operated by Kindred Spruce ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Taker AI on iOS devices.

By using Taker AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

02

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. These are stored securely in Firebase Authentication and used solely to identify your account.

Audio Recordings

When you record a session, your audio is temporarily transmitted to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription. Audio files are processed in transit and are not permanently stored by Taker AI or retained by our third-party processors after transcription is complete.

Notes and Tasks

Your transcriptions, AI-generated summaries, and voice tasks are stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore, associated with your personal account. This data is only accessible to you.

Usage Data

We do not collect analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising data of any kind.

04

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the core functionality of Taker AI:

  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Transcribe your audio recordings via OpenAI Whisper
  • Generate summaries and extract tasks via Anthropic Claude
  • Store your notes and tasks for your personal use

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

05

Data Retention

Your notes and tasks remain stored in your account until you delete them manually or delete your account entirely. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen within the app, which will permanently and irreversibly remove all your data from our systems.

06

Data Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase, which employs industry-standard encryption and security measures. Access to your data is restricted to your authenticated account only — no other user can access your notes or tasks.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password for your Google account.

07

Children's Privacy

Taker AI is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

08

Recording Consent

You are solely responsible for obtaining consent from all parties being recorded in accordance with applicable laws in your jurisdiction. Recording consent laws vary significantly by location — some require only one-party consent while others require all-party consent.

Kindred Spruce and Taker AI are not liable for any legal consequences arising from your failure to obtain proper recording consent.

09

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access your personal data stored in the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • Request information about how your data is processed
  • Withdraw consent by discontinuing use of the app

To delete your account and all associated data, use the Delete Account option in Settings within the app.
